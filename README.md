# Waste-Wise-

# Description : WasteWise AI is an innovative and intelligent waste management platform designed to solve the growing problem of urban waste through the power of Artificial Intelligence and smart digital technologies. The project focuses on helping people identify, segregate, recycle, and dispose of waste responsibly while promoting environmental sustainability and smart city development. It is specially designed for urban areas like Mumbai, where waste generation is increasing rapidly due to population growth and industrialization. 
The platform uses AI-powered image recognition technology to detect different types of waste materials by simply uploading an image or using a live camera. Once the waste image is scanned, the system analyzes it and classifies it into categories such as organic waste, plastic waste, metal waste, biomedical waste, electronic waste, and hazardous waste. Along with identification, the system also provides important information such as hazard level, recyclability, bin color recommendation, environmental impact, and proper disposal methods. This helps users make better waste management decisions and reduces the chances of improper disposal. 
WasteWise AI also includes several smart features that make waste management more efficient and user-friendly. One of its major features is the Recycling Centre Locator, which helps users find nearby recycling centers and waste collection points using location services and interactive maps. This encourages people to recycle waste instead of throwing it away carelessly. Another useful feature is the Waste Collection Scheduler, where users can schedule waste pickups, track collection history, and receive reminders for proper waste disposal. 
The platform also supports Bulk Waste Analysis, allowing organizations, industries, or housing societies to upload multiple waste images for batch processing and generate detailed waste assessment reports. In addition, the Recycling Marketplace feature promotes a circular economy by allowing users to buy and sell recyclable materials such as plastic, paper, glass, metal, and e-waste. This not only reduces waste but also creates economic opportunities for recyclers and scrap dealers. 
To increase user engagement, WasteWise AI introduces a Gamification and Rewards System called the “Waste Warrior Program.” Users can earn points, badges, rewards, and rankings by participating in waste reduction challenges and responsible recycling activities. This makes environmental awareness more interactive and motivating, especially for students and young users. The platform also includes educational tips, community participation features, and emergency reporting tools for illegal dumping or hazardous waste incidents. 
The website is designed with a modern, responsive, and eco-friendly interface using advanced HTML, CSS, and JavaScript technologies. It provides smooth navigation, animated visuals, real-time interaction, and mobile-friendly support. Overall, WasteWise AI is a complete smart waste management solution that combines Artificial Intelligence, sustainability, recycling awareness, and community participation to build a cleaner, greener, and healthier environment for future generations.
